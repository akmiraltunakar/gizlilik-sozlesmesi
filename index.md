<h2 id="privacy-policy-english">🇬🇧 Privacy Policy (English)</h2>

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026

**Developer Sim: Idle Clicker** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our application named **Developer Sim: Idle Clicker**, and its associated subdomains. By accessing or using our Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

### 1. Information We Collect
*   **Information you provide:** We do not collect any personal identity data (such as real names, phone numbers, or email addresses).
*   **Weekly Leaderboards:** To provide competitive weekly rankings, your custom startup name, country code, and weekly in-game score are stored anonymously on Firebase Realtime Database.
*   **Local Notifications:** Smart notifications are scheduled locally on your device (e.g. for vault full completion and weekly resets) without using remote push servers.
*   **Information collected automatically:** We may collect analytics and crash logs to improve the game experience.

We use the following third-party services which may collect information used to identify you:
*   [AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers
We show rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, Identifier for Advertisers on iOS), IP address, approximate location derived from that IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. This data is used to deliver and measure advertising and to detect fraud and invalid activity.

You can limit this at any time:
*   **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads, then "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
*   **iOS:** on first launch the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
*   You can also manage your choices at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and read [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 4. Leaderboard Data and Sharing
To take part in the weekly leaderboard, the app creates an anonymous account (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) that is not linked to your name, email, or phone number. Your chosen startup name, approximate country (derived from your device language), an anonymous identifier, and your weekly score are stored in Firebase Realtime Database and are shown to other players on the leaderboard.

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with Google (AdMob) for advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. We require third parties that receive data through the app to protect it to the same standard described in this policy.

### 5. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland
Where required by law, consent for the use of cookies or device identifiers for personalised advertising is requested through Google's consent mechanism. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time. For more information, see [Google's partner policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Weekly leaderboard scores are reset at the start of every week. Your startup-name reservation and anonymous identifier are kept while the app is installed. To request deletion of your leaderboard data, email us at vahitakmir-altunakar@hotmail.com and we will remove your entry.

### 7. Data Security
All data transmitted between the app and our services (Firebase, AdMob) is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

### 8. Children
